**ShortKit-ML** — our unifying ontology for shortcut detection in machine learning — is in review at *IEEE Access*. Co-authored with **Sebastian Cajas** (first author) and **Dr. Leo Anthony Celi** (last author) through the MIT Critical Data consortium.

The contribution I'm most proud of: a single ontology that bridges *bioevolutionary* and *machine-learning* interpretations of shortcut learning. It's the kind of move that only works if you read both literatures, which is the bet of the dual major.

The framework spans **20+ detection methods** benchmarked on **CheXpert, MIMIC-CXR, and CelebA**. More to come as the rest of the pipeline of manuscripts lands.
