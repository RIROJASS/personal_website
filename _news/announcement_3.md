**ShortKit-ML** — a unified multi-perspective framework for detecting and mitigating shortcut learning in medical imaging embeddings — is in review at *IEEE Access*, with the preprint posted on *medRxiv*. An eleven-author paper led by **Sebastian Cajas** (first author) through the MIT Critical Data consortium, with **Dr. Leo Anthony Celi** as PI (last author).

My contribution was the **unifying ontology** that bridges *bioevolutionary* and *machine-learning* interpretations of shortcut learning — the kind of move that only works if you read both literatures, which is the bet of the dual major.

The framework combines **20+ detection methods** with **6 mitigation techniques** under one API, benchmarked on **CheXpert, MIMIC-CXR, and CelebA** and released on **PyPI** under MIT License.
