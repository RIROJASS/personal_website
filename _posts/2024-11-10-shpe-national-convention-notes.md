Four days at the **SHPE National Convention 2024**. The database side of the convention was my own [project](/projects/13_project/); this post is just the attendee's notebook.

## Workshops that stuck

- **Amazon Career Readiness Talk** — concrete framing on how to walk into technical interviews with a story, not just a résumé.
- **Professional Networking Event** — standard playbook, but a good forcing function for practicing the 30-second version of my current work.
- **L3Harris Goal Setting 101** — simple structure, held up under pressure.
- **Northrop Grumman LGBT Leadership Panel** — honest conversations about defense culture that you don't get from the recruiting page.
- **John Deere and Automation** — the AgTech crossover with my remote-sensing work was bigger than I'd expected.

## Who I met

Representatives from **Google, Apple, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman**, and a long list of smaller firms. The Northrop line turned into [the Mission Systems internship](/cv/) the following summer, which is probably the single highest-leverage connection I've made at any convention.

## What I took back

- A better **pitch** — specifically, how to compress the dual-major into one sentence that *doesn't* read as "two unrelated hobbies."
- Realistic framing on **managing multiple responsibilities** and avoiding the overcommit trap that eats most student-leaders by senior year.
- A clearer sense of how **defense interviews** differ from standard SWE interviews — different questions, different trust-building, different pacing.
- The read that *passion + expertise + character* is the only package deal that cuts through at a 15k-person event.
