  1. ShortKit-ML
    ShortKit-ML: A Unifying Ontology for Shortcut Detection in Machine Learning
    Sebastian Cajas, Raúl Rojas, Leo Anthony Celi, and 1 more author
    IEEE Access (in review). Status: in review at IEEE Access. , 2026
    Abs Bib

    ShortKit-ML proposes a unifying ontology for shortcut detection in machine learning, bridging anthropological and ML interpretations of "shortcut." The framework spans 20+ detection methods and is benchmarked on CheXpert, MIMIC-CXR, and CelebA. Raúl contributed the unifying ontology that bridges bioevolutionary and ML readings of shortcut learning.
