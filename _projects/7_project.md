**funQ** is a startup I founded *(Jan 2025 — present)* targeting an underserved market: **real-time nightlife discovery**.

## The pitch

Yelp tells you which bar is good *in general*. Instagram tells you what your friends did *yesterday*. Neither tells you where the *good night* is happening *right now*. funQ does — by stitching together social-media data pipelines, **sentiment analysis**, and **geolocation** features into a single recommendation surface.

## The build

- **Social ingestion** — a pipeline that pulls public signal from the platforms that matter for the venue category.
- **Sentiment scoring** — per-venue, per-window, with decay (energy at a bar is a fast-moving signal).
- **Geolocation + ranking** — a recommendation layer that combines proximity, freshness, and sentiment.

Solo-founded and still in build. The project is also one of the case studies driving my interest in **agentic systems** — many of the architecture lessons feed back into [Astra](/projects/1_project/).
