As **Corporate Database Director** for the **[Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers (SHPE)](https://shpe.org/)**, I was one of five people nationally responsible for building the comprehensive record of corporate partners attending the **2024 National Convention**.

## Reach

- Distributed to **35,000+ individuals** — **15,000+** National Convention attendees and **20,000+** SHPE members.
- Covered **42 states**.
- Was *the* resource that walked members from a cold-call to a career conversation on the convention floor.

## What I actually did

Equipping the people I came up with to succeed at the convention was the kind of work that still makes me a little speechless. I owned creative direction (art style, layout decisions), the data pipeline that kept the entries current, and the delivery format itself. Pound-for-pound, probably the most-read thing I've ever shipped.

**Stack:** Photoshop, Adobe Acrobat, Canva, Excel.
