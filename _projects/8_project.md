**Art Beyond Sight** was a hackathon prototype *(October 2025)*: an accessibility mobile app that converts **visual art into soundscapes** so that visually impaired users can experience museum and gallery work auditorily.

## How it works

- **AI image analysis** parses an artwork — composition, palette, line, motion — into a structured description.
- **Audio generation** maps that structured description into a layered soundscape: instrumentation for color, rhythm for line, density for crowdedness, dynamics for contrast.
- The result is a sonic object designed not as a *replacement* for the painting but as a parallel work that gives a blind visitor real ground to interpret from.

## Why this matters to me

The Florida Museum work I did the year before pushed me to think about accessibility as a first-order design problem, not a checkbox. Art Beyond Sight is the same instinct, smaller, faster, and a lot more fun to build in 36 hours.
